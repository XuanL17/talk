Why "Calibrate"? One Construct, Many Paradigms

Conformity
a.k.a. follow-the-crowd
/ bandwagon
Autokinetic effect Sherif 1935
ambiguous moving light · group estimates converge
Line judgment Asch 1951 CoBRA
unambiguous lines · confederate majority
Private booths Crutchfield 1955
simulated majority on a panel · no face-to-face
Hotel towel reuse Goldstein et al. 2008 CoBRA
descriptive-norm sign · field behavior
Sidewalk crowd Milgram et al. 1969
passers-by look up as the crowd grows
MusicLab market Salganik et al. 2006
download counts shape song popularity
No consensus on one "true" level — effects vary by paradigm, culture, even decade (Bond & Smith, 1996).
→ We calibrate with respect to a validated paradigm: "conformity = 3.6 / 4 on Asch", not "human-like conformity".
Sherif (1935) Arch. Psychol. · Asch (1951) in Groups, Leadership and Men · Crutchfield (1955) Am. Psychol. · Milgram, Bickman & Berkowitz (1969) JPSP · Salganik, Dodds & Watts (2006) Science · Goldstein, Cialdini & Griskevicius (2008) J. Consumer Res. · Bond & Smith (1996) Psychol. Bull.
