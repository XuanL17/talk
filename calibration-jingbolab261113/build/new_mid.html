Why Is There No Verifier for Human Behavior?

1
Scarce human data
Studies are slow, costly, IRB-bound, small-sample — and rarely shared in reusable form.
2
No single right answer
Behavior is a distribution across people and contexts; "correct" lives at the level of effects.
→ No verifier → no closed loop → "looks plausible".

When Human Data Enters the Loop, Agents Improve

Centaur Nature 2025
Fine-tuned on Psych-101: 160 experiments, 60K participants, 10.7M choices.
Beats cognitive models on held-out participants.
1,000 People Park et al. 2024
Agents built from in-depth interviews with 1,052 real people.
85% as accurate as people's own 2-week test–retest on GSS.
The bottleneck is the missing human-grounded loop, not the model.
Binz et al. (2025). A foundation model to predict and capture human cognition. Nature 644. · Park et al. (2024). Generative Agent Simulations of 1,000 People. arXiv:2411.10109.

Our View: Experiments Are the Verifiers for Human Behavior

Protocol
Exact instructions, stimuli, conditions — like a well-posed problem.
Human ground truth
Validated effects and distributions — like an answer key.
Statistical test
Same analysis on agent data → a measurable gap — like a unit test.
→ Closed-loop calibration for agent behavior. First instance: CoBRA.
