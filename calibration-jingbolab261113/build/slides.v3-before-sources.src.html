Why Is Closing the Loop Hard?

1
No single right answer
Behavior is a distribution that shifts across people, contexts, and cultures.
2
Scarce ground truth
Studies are small, full personal data is near-impossible to get, and characters live in stories.
3
Judged by eye
Believability is checked by looking at it: “seems right”.
→ No verifier → the loop is never closed.

Our View: Experiments Are the Verifiers for Human Behavior

Protocol
Exact instructions, stimuli, conditions — like a well-posed problem.
Human ground truth
Validated effects for that paradigm — like an answer key (one of many, not "the" truth).
Statistical test
Same analysis on agent data → a measurable gap — like a unit test.
